About

Where complexity
becomes clarity

Refinaxa is a boutique data science consulting practice founded on a simple belief: the value of data is not in its volume, but in what you can learn from it.

We work across industries including finance, real estate, supply chain, sports analytics, and education, bringing the same rigor and clarity to every engagement. Our work spans unsupervised and supervised machine learning, with a focus on models that are not just accurate, but interpretable and actionable.

Every project is grounded in a real business problem. We deliver analysis that decision-makers can understand and use, not just results that look impressive in a notebook.

Services

What we do

From exploratory analysis through production-ready models, we help organizations at every stage of their data journey.

01
Predictive Modeling

Classification, regression, and time series models built for interpretability and real-world deployment. We translate business questions into modeling problems and deliver results that make sense to stakeholders.

02
Segmentation & Clustering

Uncover hidden structure in your data. We apply clustering, PCA, and association rule learning to segment customers, products, markets, and more into actionable groups.

03
Analytics & Reporting

Clear, audience-aware reporting that bridges technical analysis and business decision-making. We specialize in turning complex findings into plain-language recommendations.

04
Neural Networks & Deep Learning

Autoencoders, LSTM forecasting, and neural network classifiers for problems where traditional methods fall short. Applied to demand forecasting, anomaly detection, and recommendation systems.

05
Data Pipeline & Preparation

Clean, analysis-ready data is the foundation of every good model. We build preprocessing pipelines, handle feature engineering, and ensure your data is structured for the problem at hand.

06
Domain-Specific Analytics

Specialized experience in healthcare, finance, real estate, supply chain, and sports analytics. We bring domain knowledge alongside technical rigor to every engagement.
